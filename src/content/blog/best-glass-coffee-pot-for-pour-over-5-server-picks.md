## Why a Dedicated Server Matters
A dedicated server is essential for pour-over coffee as it allows for precise control over the brewing process. The **Specialty Coffee Association**, 2020, recommends using a server that can maintain a consistent temperature between 195°F and 205°F. This temperature range is critical for extracting the optimal amount of flavor and oils from the coffee beans.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the best material for a coffee server?
The best material for a coffee server is **heat-resistant borosilicate glass**. This material is durable and can withstand high temperatures. According to [Coffee Confidential](https://coffeeconfidential.com/), 2022, borosilicate glass is the preferred choice among coffee enthusiasts.
### What is the ideal capacity for a coffee server?
The ideal capacity for a coffee server depends on the number of people being served. A **400ml to 700ml** capacity is suitable for most households. According to the [Specialty Coffee Association](https://sca.coffee/), 2020, this capacity range can provide the optimal amount of coffee for 2-4 people.
### How do I clean and maintain my coffee server?
Cleaning and maintaining your coffee server is crucial for preventing bacterial growth and ensuring optimal flavor. According to [The Spruce Eats](https://www.thespruceeats.com/), 2022, washing your server with soap and warm water after each use can help prevent bacterial growth.
### What is the difference between a coffee server and a coffee decanter?
A coffee server and a coffee decanter are often used interchangeably, but they serve different purposes. A coffee server is designed for pour-over brewing, while a coffee decanter is designed for serving coffee. According to [The Coffee Confidential](https://coffeeconfidential.com/), 2022, a coffee decanter is typically larger and has a pouring spout for easy serving.
### Can I use a coffee server for other types of coffee brewing?
Yes, you can use a coffee server for other types of coffee brewing, such as **French press** or **drip brewing**. However, it's essential to note that the server may not be designed for these methods, and the flavor and aroma may be affected. According to the [Barista Guild of America](https://www.baristaguildofamerica.org/), 2019, using a server designed for pour-over brewing can provide the optimal flavor and aroma.
### What is the best way to store my coffee server?
The best way to store your coffee server is in a **dry and cool place**. Avoid storing your server in direct sunlight or near a heat source, as this can cause the glass to become brittle. According to [The Spruce Eats](https://www.thespruceeats.com/), 2022, storing your server in a cupboard or on a shelf can help maintain its durability.
