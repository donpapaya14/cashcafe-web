## What beginners need
When it comes to finding the **best espresso machine for beginners**, key features such as simple interfaces and **automatic temperature control** are crucial. According to the [Specialty Coffee Association](https://sca.coffee/), 2022, a good beginner espresso machine should have an easy-to-use interface and maintain a consistent temperature between **195°F and 205°F**. This ensures that the espresso is extracted correctly and tastes great.

## Pressurized vs non-pressurized baskets
When it comes to espresso machines, there are two types of baskets: **pressurized and non-pressurized**. According to the [Barista Guild of America](https://www.baristaguildofamerica.org/), 2020, pressurized baskets are great for beginners because they are more forgiving and can produce a good shot of espresso even with inconsistent grind sizes. However, non-pressurized baskets offer more control over the brewing process and can produce a more nuanced flavor.

## Built-in grinder yes or no
Some espresso machines come with a **built-in grinder**, which can be convenient for beginners. According to [The Sprudge Coffee Guide](https://sprudge.com/), 2024, built-in grinders can be a great option for those who want a simple and easy-to-use machine. However, they can also be more expensive and may not offer as much control over the grind size as a separate grinder.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the standard pump pressure for an espresso machine?
The standard pump pressure for an espresso machine is **9 bar**. This is according to [Espresso Machine Review](https://espressomachinereview.com/), 2023.
### How long does it take for an espresso machine to heat up?
The heat-up time for an espresso machine can vary, but most machines take around **5-10 minutes** to reach the optimal temperature.
### What is the best grind size for espresso?
The best grind size for espresso is **fine to medium-fine**, according to [James Hoffmann's 2020 ratio test](https://www.jimseven.com/).
### Can I use a blade grinder to grind my coffee beans?
No, it's not recommended to use a **blade grinder** to grind your coffee beans. Blade grinders can generate heat and damage the beans, which can affect the flavor of your espresso.
### How often should I clean my espresso machine?
You should clean your espresso machine **daily** to prevent the buildup of old coffee oils and residue. This will help to maintain the flavor of your espresso and prevent any bacterial growth.
