## Burr Material: Steel vs Ceramic
When it comes to **burr material**, there are two main options: steel and ceramic. According to the Barista Guild of America (2019), steel burrs are more durable and resistant to wear, but can generate heat and damage the coffee beans. Ceramic burrs, on the other hand, are more prone to wear and tear, but are less likely to generate heat and damage the beans. Here is a comparison table:
| Burr Material | Durability | Heat Generation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steel | High | High |
| Ceramic | Low | Low |

### How do I clean and maintain my manual coffee grinder?
To clean and maintain your manual coffee grinder, simply wipe it down with a dry cloth and avoid exposing it to water or moisture. You can also use a soft brush to clean out any coffee grounds or debris.
### What is the difference between a manual and electric coffee grinder?
A manual coffee grinder uses a hand-crank to grind the coffee beans, while an electric coffee grinder uses a motor to grind the beans. Manual grinders are often more affordable and offer a more flavorful cup, while electric grinders are faster and more convenient.
### Can I use a manual coffee grinder for espresso?
Yes, you can use a manual coffee grinder for espresso, but it may require more effort and time to grind the beans to the correct fineness. It is recommended to use a grinder with adjustable grind settings to find the perfect grind for your espresso.
### How often should I replace my manual coffee grinder burrs?
