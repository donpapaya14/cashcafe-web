## Conical vs Flat at This Price
Most under-$200 electric models use **38-40mm conical burrs**, which have advantages and disadvantages compared to flat burrs. Barista Hustle (2019) notes that conical burrs are more common in this price range due to their lower cost and easier manufacturing process. However, flat burrs can provide more consistent grind quality and are often preferred by professional baristas.
