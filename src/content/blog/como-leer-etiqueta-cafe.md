90% of supermarket coffee bags say nothing useful. Learn to read a specialty coffee label and never buy blind again.

## The essentials

1. Roast date — NOT best before. If it's missing, don't buy it.
2. Country and region of origin — 'Colombia Huila' is useful. '100% Arabica' says nothing.
3. Botanical variety — Caturra, Bourbon, Geisha, SL28… each tastes different.
4. Process — Washed (clean), natural (fruity), honey (sweet). Changes everything.
5. Roast level — Light, medium, dark. Choose based on your brew method.

## Red flags

- Only says '100% Arabica' with no detail — empty marketing.
- Best-before date but no roast date — probably months old.
- Blend with no specified origins — hiding bad coffee.
- Pretty label but zero technical info — all image.

## Example of a good label

Finca La Esperanza · Huila, Colombia · Caturra variety · Washed process · Medium roast · Roasted 15/03/2026 · Notes: milk chocolate, plum, caramel · SCA score: 86. That's transparency.

> If the roaster won't print the roast date, ask yourself what else they're hiding.
