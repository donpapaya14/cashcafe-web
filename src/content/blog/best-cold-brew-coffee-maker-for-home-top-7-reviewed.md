## How Cold Brew Makers Differ
Cold brew makers can be made from various materials, including **glass**, **plastic**, and **stainless steel**. Each material has its own set of advantages and disadvantages. For instance, glass is non-porous and easy to clean, while stainless steel is durable and resistant to corrosion. The Specialty Coffee Association (2020) recommends considering the brewing method and features when choosing a cold brew maker.

## Mesh vs Paper Filtration
When it comes to **cold brew pitcher review**, one of the key considerations is the type of filtration used. **Mesh** filters are known for their ability to allow more oils and sediment to pass through, resulting in a richer and more full-bodied flavor. On the other hand, **paper** filters are designed to absorb more of the coffee's natural oils and sediment, resulting in a cleaner and smoother flavor. According to James Hoffmann (2018), the choice between mesh and paper filters ultimately comes down to personal preference.
