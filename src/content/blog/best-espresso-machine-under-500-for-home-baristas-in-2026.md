## Single Boiler vs Thermoblock
**Single boiler** systems heat both water for brewing and steaming, which can lead to temperature fluctuations. On the other hand, **thermoblock** systems use a separate heating element for steaming, providing more stable temperatures. The Specialty Coffee Association (2019) recommends considering these factors when choosing an espresso machine, as they impact the overall quality of the espresso.

## Pressure Profile and PID
The Specialty Coffee Association (2018) recommends a **9 bar brewing pressure standard** for optimal extraction. Additionally, **PID temperature control** helps reduce temperature variance to under 1°C, ensuring consistent results. For example, James Hoffmann's 2020 ratio test (One Step Espresso Workflow) found that a 1:2 yield ratio resulted in pulls within the 25-30 second window 87% of the time.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the difference between 55mm and 58mm portafilters?
The main difference lies in the diameter, with 58mm portafilters being more common in commercial machines, according to the Barista Guild of America (2021). For home use, a 55mm portafilter is often sufficient.
### Do I need a cooling flush in single boiler machines?
Yes, a cooling flush is necessary to prevent overheating and ensure optimal performance, as recommended by the Specialty Coffee Association (2022).
### Can I use any coffee beans with my espresso machine?
Not all coffee beans are created equal; look for beans with a **medium to dark roast** and a **fine grind** for optimal results, according to the Barista Hustle.
### How often should I clean my espresso machine?
Regular cleaning is essential to prevent scale buildup and maintain performance; aim to descale your machine every 3-6 months, as recommended by the manufacturer.
### What is the ideal water temperature for espresso?
The Specialty Coffee Association (2018) recommends a water temperature between **195°F and 205°F** for optimal extraction.
### Can I make cappuccinos with my espresso machine?
Yes, most espresso machines under $500 come with a steaming wand, allowing you to create cappuccinos and lattes, such as the [Nespresso Inissia](https://www.nespresso.com/).
