## What you compromise at this price
When opting for an **espresso machine under $200**, there are several compromises to consider. Thermoblock heating, for instance, can heat water quickly but may lack the temperature stability of a boiler (Specialty Coffee Association, 2020). Material quality and design also play a significant role in the overall performance and durability of the machine.

### Material Quality and Design Compromises
- **Plastic components**: More common in budget machines, which can affect durability and taste.
- **Simplified designs**: May lack advanced features but can be easier to use and maintain.

## Pressurized basket reality
**Pressurized baskets** can significantly affect the quality of the espresso and the user experience. According to Barista Hustle (2019), pressurized baskets can help with puck formation but may also lead to over-extraction and a less nuanced flavor profile.

### Tips for Using Pressurized Baskets
1. **Use the right coffee**: Choose a coffee that complements the pressurized basket's characteristics.
2. **Adjust grind size**: Experiment with grind sizes to find the optimal setting for your machine.

## When to upgrade later
As you become more familiar with espresso and your machine, you may find that you've outgrown your entry-level **budget espresso machine**. Signs that it's time to upgrade include a desire for more advanced features, such as temperature control or a built-in grinder (CoffeeGeek, 2021).

### Signs You've Outgrown Your Machine

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the best cheap espresso machine under $200?
The best cheap espresso machine under $200 can vary depending on your specific needs and preferences. However, popular options include the Mr. Coffee Cafe Barista and the Nespresso Inissia.
### How do I clean my espresso machine?
Regular cleaning is essential for maintaining your espresso machine's performance and longevity. Refer to your machine's manual for specific cleaning instructions, and consider descaling every 3-6 months (Reddit r/Coffee, 2022).
### Can I use any coffee beans in my espresso machine?
While you can use any coffee beans, the quality and roast level can significantly impact the flavor of your espresso. Look for beans that are freshly roasted and have a medium to dark roast level for optimal flavor (The Spruce Eats, 2022).
### How often should I descale my espresso machine?
Descaling your espresso machine every 3-6 months can help prevent mineral buildup and maintain its performance (Reddit r/Coffee, 2022).
### What is the difference between a thermoblock and a boiler?
A thermoblock heats water quickly but may lack temperature stability, whereas a boiler provides more stable temperatures but can be slower to heat (Specialty Coffee Association, 2020).
### Can I make cappuccinos with a budget espresso machine?
Yes, many budget espresso machines come with steam wands that allow you to froth milk and make cappuccinos. However, the quality of the frothing may vary depending on the machine (The Spruce Eats, 2022).
