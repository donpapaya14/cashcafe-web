## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the best naked portafilter brand for beginners?
The **IMS Naked Portafilter** is an excellent choice for beginners, offering high-quality construction and compatibility with 58mm machines. As noted by [CoffeeGeek](https://www.coffeegeek.com/), the IMS Naked Portafilter is a popular choice among coffee enthusiasts (CoffeeGeek, 2022).
### How do I choose the right basket size for my naked portafilter?
Choose a basket size that is compatible with your machine and the desired dose. For example, the **VST 58mm Basket** is designed for use with 14-22g doses.
### Can I use a naked portafilter with any espresso machine?
Not all espresso machines are compatible with naked portafilters. Check your machine's compatibility before purchasing a naked portafilter.
### What is the difference between a naked portafilter and a standard portafilter?
A naked portafilter has no spouts or valves, allowing for a more nuanced flavor profile. According to [James Hoffmann](https://www.jameshoffmann.com/), naked portafilters can provide a more **even extraction** and **optimal flavor** (James Hoffmann, 2020).
### How do I clean and maintain my naked portafilter?
Regularly clean and descale your naked portafilter to prevent buildup and maintain optimal performance.
### Can I use a naked portafilter with a super-automatic espresso machine?
Most super-automatic espresso machines are not compatible with naked portafilters. Check your machine's compatibility before purchasing a naked portafilter.
