## Introduction to Espresso Without a Machine
To make **espresso without a machine**, you need to understand what true espresso requires. According to the [Specialty Coffee Association](https://sca.coffee/), true espresso needs **9 bar pressure**, which significantly impacts the flavor and crema of the espresso.

## What Espresso Really Requires
True espresso requires a specific set of conditions, including **9 bar pressure**, to produce the distinctive flavor and crema. As stated by the Specialty Coffee Association (2020), this pressure is crucial for achieving the perfect shot.

## Moka Pot Method
The Moka pot is a popular alternative for making espresso without a machine. It produces strong, concentrated coffee at **1.5-2 bar**, which is lower than the required 9 bar. However, with the right brewing technique and ratio, you can still achieve a great cup. James Hoffmann, World Barista Champion (2017), recommends a 1:7 to 1:9 coffee-to-water ratio for the Moka pot.

### Moka Pot Brewing Technique
To brew with a Moka pot, follow these steps:
1. Add **18-20 grams** of coffee to the filter basket.
2. Use **300-320 ml** of water.
3. Heat the Moka pot over **medium heat**.
4. Serve immediately.

## AeroPress Espresso Style
The AeroPress can produce concentrated coffee, but it's not technically espresso. However, by adjusting the brewing parameters, you can achieve a similar result. According to the AeroPress official brewing guide (2019), use **17-20 grams** of coffee and **240-260 ml** of water to achieve a strong, espresso-like brew.

### AeroPress Brewing Parameters
To brew with an AeroPress, follow these steps:
1. Add **17-20 grams** of coffee to the chamber.
2. Use **240-260 ml** of water.
3. Stir and steep for **1-2 minutes**.
4. Press the mixture through the filter.
5. Serve immediately.
